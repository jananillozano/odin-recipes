Iced Chocolate

description

A refreshing chocolate drink made with milk, cocoa powder, and ice.

ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Ice cubes

instructions

  1. In a small saucepan, heat the milk over medium heat until warm but not boiling.
  2. In a separate bowl, mix the cocoa powder and sugar together.
  3. Add the cocoa mixture to the warm milk and whisk until fully combined.
    4. Remove from heat and stir in the vanilla extract.
  4. Fill a glass with ice cubes and pour the chocolate milk over the ice.
  5. Stir well and enjoy your refreshing iced chocolate!
